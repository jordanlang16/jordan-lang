
# A/B Test Plan: Study Space Website

Website: https://jordanlang16.github.io/jordan-lang/index.html

## 1. Page and Element Being Tested

The page being tested is my study space listing page, which highlights quiet places to study in Iowa City. The goal of the page is to encourage college students to click into the website and explore the study spaces.

The one element I am changing is the noise level rating displayed for each study space.

- **Version A (Current Page):** Each study space has a noise level rating from 1-5 displayed with its heading.
- **Version B (New Page):** The noise level ratings are removed. Everything else stays the same including the introductory paragraph, bold study space headings, and tips for finding quiet study spaces at the bottom of the page.

## 2. Hypothesis and Reasoning

I believe Version A will receive a higher percentage of positive responses than Version B. My reasoning is that college students looking for quiet places to study before an exam may find noise level ratings helpful because they can quickly compare locations and decide which spaces are worth exploring. Without these ratings, visitors may have less information to help them make a decision.

## 3. Model, Persona, Prompt, and Assignment

I will use ChatGPT-6 for all 100 trials, keeping the model and settings consistent throughout the experiment. Each trial will take place in a new conversation with no memory of previous trials.

**Persona:** A college student in Iowa City looking for quiet study spaces to visit before a big exam.

**Prompt (same for every trial):**

You are a college student in Iowa City looking for quiet study spaces to attend before a big exam. Therefore, you are looking on websites to see the reviews for the study spaces in the area. Read this space listing website to decide whether you would click into that study spaces website or not. Answer yes or no and give one sentence of reasoning.

The only difference between trials will be whether the AI visitor sees Version A or Version B of the website. I will randomly assign visitors to each version before running the trials, using a random coin flip each time. Heads are Version A and tails are Version B. Also, with 50 visitors assigned to each version.

## 4. Primary Outcome and Measurement

The primary outcome will be whether the AI visitor says they would click into the study space website. Each response will be recorded as either "yes" or "no." I will calculate the percentage of yes responses for each version to determine which page encourages more visitors to explore the website.

## 5. Sample Size and Timeline

I will complete 100 total trials, with 50 visitors viewing Version A and 50 viewing Version B. I plan to complete all trials by November 16th, 2026, so I have time to review and organize the results before submitting the assignment.

## 6. Comparison and Expected Results

I will compare the percentage of yes responses between Version A and Version B. If Version A receives a higher percentage of yes responses, that would support my hypothesis that noise level ratings encourage students to explore the study spaces. If Version B performs better, or both versions receive similar results, my hypothesis would not be supported.
