Quiet Study Spots in Iowa City

Some assignments need silence: proofs, essays, and anything with a citation style. These fictional spots are the calmest we found, each given a noise rating from 1 (library hush) to 5 (busy café).

Noise: 1 of 5

The Silent Annex at Hawthorn House

A glass-walled room where talking is not allowed, even in whispers. Desks have dividers and small lamps. Because it is so popular, the staff offer a free two-hour reservation each morning at 8 a.m.

Noise: 1 of 5

Cedar Stacks Reading Hall

Rows of single desks tucked between tall bookshelves, which absorb sound surprisingly well. The overhead lights are bright, so bring a sweater, because the room runs cool.

Noise: 2 of 5

Quillfeather Tea Room, Back Corner

The front is gently chatty, but the back corner has four soft chairs and almost no foot traffic. Order a pot of tea and settle in. Music stays instrumental and low.

Noise: 2 of 5

Meadowlight Chapel Study Nook

A small, calm room with wooden pews and wide windows, open to students during the day. Typing is fine, but phone calls are not. It is peaceful, if a little chilly in January.

Tips for finding silence anywhere

Arrive early, because quiet seats vanish by mid-morning. Sit away from doors, restrooms, and counters. Noise-canceling headphones help, but even foam earplugs lower a room by a surprising amount. If a spot gets loud, move rather than fight it.
