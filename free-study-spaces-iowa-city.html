Free Study Spaces in Iowa City

Textbooks are expensive enough. These fictional spaces cost nothing to use, require no purchase, and welcome long stays. We note the hours and the catch, if any, so you are never surprised at the door.

Free · Open 8 a.m. to 9 p.m.

Ironwood Community Library, Second Floor

Big tables, free Wi-Fi, and printers that charge only a few cents per page. The catch: weekend afternoons fill fast, so arrive by noon.

Free · Open 24 hours in finals week

Foxglove Student Union Lounge

Couches, tables, and plenty of outlets. It is busy and a bit noisy, but the energy helps when motivation runs low. Vending machines are nearby.

Free · Open 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.

Larkspur Neighborhood Center Study Room

A small, calm room run by volunteers, with a kettle and free instant coffee packets. Sign in at the front desk. Quiet hours are enforced, which we appreciated.

Free · Sunrise to sunset

Willowmere Park Pavilion

Covered picnic tables near a pond. It works best in mild weather and there is no Wi-Fi, so it is ideal for reading and handwritten notes.

Making a free spot work for you

Pack a charger, a water bottle, and snacks so you are never tempted to leave. Be kind to the space by cleaning up after yourself, since free places stay free only when people respect them.

If you have a few dollars to spare, our best study spots are worth a visit. For silence, try the quiet study spots.
