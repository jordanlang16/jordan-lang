Find your focus in Iowa City

Corner Desk Guide was started by two friends, Maya Okafor and Dev Lindqvist, after a finals week spent wandering from full table to full table with a dying laptop. We visit study spots, test the outlets and the Wi-Fi, and tell you honestly where you will actually get work done.

How we rate a spot

Every location gets the same four checks: seating comfort, outlet access, noise level, and how welcoming the staff are to people who stay a while. We visit at least twice, once on a weekday morning and once in the evening, because a place can feel completely different by dinner time.

Meet the team

Maya Okafor is a junior who studies best in the early morning and has strong opinions about chairs. Dev Lindqvist is a senior who prefers a busy hum and has never met a free outlet he did not like.
